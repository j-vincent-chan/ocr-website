Our approach

Data managers who sit with your team.

Good data depend on the people who collect and use them. We work alongside investigators, coordinators, and project managers rather than downstream of them.

  • Investigators

    Define the questions the data must answer, and review what the data show along the way.

  • Clinical research coordinators

    Collect and enter the data. We give them workflows, checks, and discrepancy reports they can act on.

  • Project managers

    Track milestones. We report data completeness and quality on the same schedule.

  • Analysts and collaborators

    Receive documented, analysis-ready datasets and the data dictionaries that explain them.

Where this service stops

Database engineering, LIMS implementation, EHR infrastructure, bioinformatics, and AI/ML are outside this service. When a project needs them, we coordinate with the UCSF groups that provide them.Confirm wording
