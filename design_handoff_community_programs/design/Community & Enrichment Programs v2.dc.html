Where this arm fits

People and programming, not project management.

Community management owns the people, relationships, engagement, and programming of a research community. The scientific and operational management of research projects belongs to OCR's Research Operations & Management arm. On a large award, the two work side by side.

Four ways to engage

Scaled to the community, not the other way around.

The same team, four shapes. Most partners start with one and grow into another as the community does.

  1. Model 01

    Partial effort on a center grant

    A share of an OCR community manager, budgeted into the Enrichment or Administrative Core, runs the program on the grant's calendar and produces the reporting the center requires.

    In practice
    Diabetes Center DRCDiabetes Center NORC
  2. Model 02

    Embedded full-time community manager

    A dedicated person hired through OCR and placed inside the community: owning the retreat, the seminar series, the newsletter, the award programs, and the relationships.

    In practice
    Bakar ImmunoX Initiative
  3. Model 03

    Shared coordination across institutes

    One manager working across several institutes or programs, so each gets experienced coverage and the group gets a common calendar, a shared fund, and a voice with campus leadership.

    In practice
    Parnassus Institutes
  4. Model 04

    Written into the grant with you

    Before the application goes in, OCR drafts the enrichment or community engagement section and budget with the Enrichment Director, using programs we already run as the evidence base. When the award lands, the plan is already staffed.

    In practice
    P30 & P50 centersU54 & program projectsInstitute-level centers
